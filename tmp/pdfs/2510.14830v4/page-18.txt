At each outer iteration, we (i) train critics and a transition model on the current dataset, (ii) improve the current behavior policy via conservative offline RL updates, (iii) collect new rollouts with the improved policy, (iv) merge the new data into the dataset, and (v) re-train the diffusion policy with IL on the expanded dataset. This creates a virtuous cycle where better policies generate higher-quality data, which in turn enables learning even better policies.
